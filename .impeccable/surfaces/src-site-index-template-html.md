# Looks gallery site

Scope: the public interactive gallery, one static page (docs/index.html, built from src/site/). Mode: Experience, with a task at the end: scan many looks at once, keep some, open one, copy its install line. Visitor: someone choosing a look for the Claude desktop or mobile app. Constraints: plain static HTML, CSS and JS; GitHub Pages; data embedded at build; no invented counts, ratings or screenshots; previews are renderings and must say so.

Decided with the user: scanning many at once leads; the preview itself may be rethought; it must not read as a generic template or as too busy to scan 95 looks. Revision after the first build: the user found the look frames hard to tell apart on film strips and asked for polaroids; they chose a neat grid with a slight tilt, a handwritten name plus role-color dots on the white strip, and the black film band kept as the mat that holds the opened print.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a light table of polaroid prints: 95 looks as white-framed instant photos in a grid, each turned a degree or two, judged at a glance and kept with a grease-pencil circle. It refuses the grid of equal cards under a dark header that every theme gallery ships.

OWN-WORLD: Polaroids on a light table. A warm-neutral table surface, white instant-film frames with a thick caption strip and a soft contact shadow, a marker hand for the names, edge-print numbers in a measuring mono, a condensed grotesque for the wordmark, one grease-pencil red used only for circles, focus and the active-filter underline, and a black film mat that holds the opened print. The chrome is achromatic; the looks supply every other color.

STORY: The visitor sees all 95 prints at once and understands each is a look in use, tells them apart by the picture, the handwritten name and its five color dots, circles the ones they like, opens one into the black mat to read its exact colors, its credit and its install line, and copies the line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A header on the table (wordmark LOOKS at left, a mono roll line beneath it, the filters and search to the right) over the first rows of polaroids, about five across on a laptop, each with its picture, a handwritten name, a small frame number and five role-color dots. A loupe follows the pointer over a picture. The primary action, copying an install line, lives in the print that opens inline in a black mat under the picked polaroid's row.

FORM: Polaroid prints on a light table, evolved by the user from the proof sheet (position 1 on the ordered list); seed:22a5d861867df8d2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
