# Looks gallery site

Scope: the public interactive gallery, one static page (docs/index.html, built from src/site/). Mode: Experience, with a task at the end: scan many looks at once, keep some, open one, copy its install line. Visitor: someone choosing a look for the Claude desktop or mobile app. Constraints: plain static HTML, CSS and JS; GitHub Pages; data embedded at build; no invented counts, ratings or screenshots; previews are renderings and must say so.

Decided with the user: scanning many at once leads; the preview itself may be rethought; it must not read as a generic template or as too busy to scan 95 looks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one photographic proof sheet: 95 looks as numbered film frames in strips, judged at a glance and kept with a grease-pencil circle. It refuses the grid of equal cards under a dark header that every theme gallery ships.

OWN-WORLD: A paper contact sheet on a light table. Warm-neutral paper, black film strips with sprocket holes, amber edge-print numbers in a measuring mono, a condensed grotesque for the wordmark and film labels, and one grease-pencil red used only for circles and focus. The chrome is achromatic; the looks supply every other color.

STORY: The visitor sees all 95 frames at once and understands each is a look in use, circles the ones they like, opens one as a print under its strip to read its exact colors, its credit and its install line, and copies the line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A sheet header (wordmark LOOKS at left, a mono roll line beneath it, the filters and search to the right) over the first strips of frames at about 200px each, frame numbers and names printed in the black film margin. A loupe follows the pointer over a frame. The primary action, copying an install line, lives in the print that opens inline under the picked frame's strip.

FORM: A proof sheet, position 1 on the ordered list; seed:22a5d861867df8d2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
